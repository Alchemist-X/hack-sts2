### Policy (cheap, coverage-oriented)

- Combat: uses a potion when HP is below 40% or on round 1 of an elite or boss
  fight (60%).
- Cards are scored before playing:
  - powers early;
  - block when hit (estimated from the attack intents);
  - damage from the engine `rule_preview`, with a bonus for a kill;
  - 0-cost cards.
- It plays the top-scored card 90% of the time and a random card the other 10%.
  It ends the turn when it has no playable card.
- Map: weighted choice.
  - Elite weight 0.1 in act 1 before floor 8; later 1.0 when HP is above 80%, else 0.15.
  - Rest weight goes up when HP is low.
  - Shop weight goes up when gold is at least 120.
  - Unknown and Treasure rooms are favoured.
- Rest site: heal below 55% HP, otherwise smith.
- Card reward: take a random card 85% of the time.
- Shop: up to 4 random affordable purchases.
- Event: random unlocked option.
- Rewards and treasure: claim everything.

## Crashes and restarts

- 8 workers stopped answering mid-run (`crash`: the MCP was not reachable and
  the liveness probe failed). The driver restarted each one, abandoned the
  saved run and started the next run.
  - By worker: w4 4, w1 2, w2 1, w3 1.
  - By character: DEFECT 5, SILENT 2, REGENT 1.
  - Floor at the crash: 2, 7, 14, 3, 22, 5, 2, 2. The act-2 run (floor 22)
    was one of them.
  - godot.log ends with no exception in the two cases checked; one of them
    is a Defect run whose last lines are orb/Lightning card plays.
  - macOS wrote crash reports for two of the deaths (02:44:24 and 03:44:55).
    Both are SIGABRT from `libcoreclr` `TerminateProcess` under
    `DispatchManagedException`: an unhandled managed exception. The
    exception text went to the worker's `game.log`, and `headless_launch.sh`
    truncates that file on relaunch, so the text is lost. `run_farm.py` now
    copies `game.log` to `<farm>/crashlogs/` before every restart.
  - The other 6 deaths left no crash report.
  - The 4 reports at 03:58:22 come from the final stop (SIGTERM, then an
    exit-time `std::terminate`). They are shutdown noise, not crashes.
  - Triage lead: the Defect share (5 of 8).
- The 5-minute no-progress (hang) restart never fired.
- 4 further `restart` events at 02:11:55-02:12:14 are shutdown artifacts: the
  driver was stopped while waiting on terminal screens. They are annotated in
  `events.jsonl`, and the driver has been fixed so this cannot recur.

### Driver restarts

The first segment (14.2 min) showed starter decks dying to act-1 elites on
floor 7. I lowered the early-elite weight and restarted the driver. The
restart also added unique run ids per segment and a clean shutdown path. A
1.3-min segment was aborted at once to add the run-id fix. All recordings from
every segment are kept and counted above.
